- Reject requires a reason; Approve/Reject remain Website-only actions.

- Gift purchases go to the user's Gift Inventory.
- Profile page displays Social V2 stats and Gift Inventory.
- Rankings adds `Người Nổi Tiếng` (Fame) and removes the legacy weekly Game Master table.
